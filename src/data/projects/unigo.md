An academic group project (5 members) for **IN 3700 Human Computer Interaction** at the University of Moratuwa. UniGo is a mobile app for finding university events, signing up for activities and keeping track of event schedules.

**Tagline:** _Less scrolling. More showing up._

## The design

Ten screens cover the full student journey, from first launch to walking into an event:

1. **Welcome:** clear "Get started" and "Log in" actions.
2. **Log in:** university email sign-in with password recovery.
3. **Create account:** sign up with name, UoM email and password.
4. **Home:** a personalised greeting, search, category filters and this week's events.
5. **Explore events:** search with quick filters (UoM, This week, Free) and sorting.
6. **Event details:** date, venue, audience and a clear "Register now" button.
7. **Reserve your place:** a short sign-up form with optional accessibility needs.
8. **Event ticket:** a confirmed booking with a QR ticket for entry.
9. **My schedule:** a weekly calendar of booked events, with reminders and a cancel option.
10. **Your profile:** university details, saved events, interests and preferences.

The prototype is fully clickable, with bottom navigation (Home, Explore, Schedule, Profile) linking the screens.

## HCI principles applied

We designed around **consistency, readability, accessibility, clear navigation and visual hierarchy**. A shared design system (colour palette, typography, icon set, spacing grid and reusable cards, buttons and navigation) keeps every screen consistent. Every screen was checked for colour contrast, text size and touch-target size.

**My contribution:** I designed the **Create Account** and **Your Profile** screens, exported the final UI images, compiled the submission document and checked that all Figma and Drive links were accessible. I also took part in planning the user flow, applying HCI principles and reviewing the final prototype with the team.
