An academic group project (Group 33, "CodeNova") built for a Computer Graphics module — a navigable 3D city with distinct zones (Residential Area, City Centre, City Park, Construction Zone, Beach/Riverside), rendered with OpenGL and driven by keyboard/mouse camera controls.

**My contribution:** I designed the industrial road view (the Construction Zone scene).
